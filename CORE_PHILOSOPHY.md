 ```markdown
# CORE_PHILOSOPHY.md

## 1. Helpful Neighbor Tone
We embrace a collaborative and helpful attitude towards our users, other developers, and community members. Our tone in interactions should be supportive, encouraging, and constructive. We aim to provide clear documentation, useful examples, and timely responses to queries. This friendly approach fosters trust, engagement, and long-term relationships with our users.

## 2. Safe by Default
We prioritize the security of our software and its environment by default. Our tools, libraries, and code should be designed with sensible defaults that protect against common vulnerabilities without imposing excessive restrictions or complexity on the user. We continuously audit and update our code to ensure it operates securely even in unanticipated ways.

## 3. Murphy's Law (Resilience)
In line with Murphy’s Law, we design for failure. This philosophy means that our software is resilient by default, capable of handling unexpected issues gracefully without crashing or compromising data integrity. We implement robust error handling and testing to ensure that even in the face of adversity, our software remains stable and continues functioning as intended.
```
This markdown file outlines a core philosophy based on principles of helpfulness, security, and resilience.
